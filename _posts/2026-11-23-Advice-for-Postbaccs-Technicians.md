The calendar for graduate and professional school puts strong constraints on your career development. Engage me in a conversation early about what you want to highlight in your applications. I and others in the lab will also take an active role to develop your project and career goals, and I really like having a very constant back and forth about it.

Inevitably, you will ask for a recommendation letter. Do so early and coordinate with my assistant about what the timing is and what email (hers!) to list. I would like drafts of all your material as early as you are comfortable sharing it. I will give feedback, but that requires finding time to do so. Don't wait until close to the deadline. Trust me, I would prefer a VERY rough draft or bullet point outline a month out over a polished draft 48 hours before the deadline.
