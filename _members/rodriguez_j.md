description: "
Josh earned his B.S. in Biochemistry from California State University, Fresno, before completing his Ph.D. in Chemistry and Chemical Biology in Michael Thompson’s lab at the University of California Merced. His doctoral research focused on drug discovery targeting the human immune protein IRF3. Using crystallographic fragment screening, he discovered novel small-molecule binding sites and identified chemical starting points for developing ligands that target IRF3. In the Fraser Lab, Josh studies proteins implicated in autism and Parkinson’s disease using structural and biophysical approaches. His work focuses on discovering small-molecule binders and developing them into potent, selective chemical probes that can modulate protein function.


Outside of the lab, Josh enjoys exploring new food and coffee spots and drawing inspiration from them for his own cooking and espresso brewing at home.
